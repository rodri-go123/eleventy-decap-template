## Limited archive preserving the four olfactory compositions developed for The Smelling Brain installation.

Format: 4 × 2 ml.

Each natural composition functions as a sensory document, preserving traces of specific atmospheres, geographies, and perceptual states.

Conceived as an extension of the exhibition, the Archive Edition approaches perfume as a medium for preserving, transmitting, and reactivating embodied sensory experience.

01 — Memory (Fougère)  
02 — Attraction (Chypre)  
03 — Flavour (Gourmand)  
04 — Affect (Hesperidic)
