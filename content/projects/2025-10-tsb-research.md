## Natural perfumery, anatomical speculation, sensory experimentation, material studies, and emotional scent composition developed through embodied olfactory research.

Natural materials, experimental accords, and olfactory structures are treated as unstable sensory matter capable of triggering memory, modulating attraction, and shaping flavour perception.

Positioned between natural perfumery, anatomical research, and speculative design, the practice approaches scent as both emotional archive and living material in continuous transformation, where compositions evolve as perceptual systems capable of encoding, transmitting, and reactivating intimate sensory information.
