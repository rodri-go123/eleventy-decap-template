## Graphic novel, A5 format, 16 pages.

WHO investigates the emotional and ethical consequences of autonomous military technologies through speculative storytelling and visual narration. Using the comic as a design research tool, the project reflects on the asymmetry of contemporary conflicts and the deployment of emotionless machines within systems of war.

Through narratives of grief, mourning, and resistance, the work questions the normalization of automated violence and the erosion of human value within technologically mediated warfare.
